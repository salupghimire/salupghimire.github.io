About

I'm a Grade 12 student in Nepal, learning by building rather than reading about it.

I study at Divya College, and most of my free time usually ends up going into coding. I spend a lot of it working with Python, building front-end interfaces, and trying to figure out why something that worked five minutes ago suddenly doesn't.

I'm still learning, so I don't want to pretend I know everything. I enjoy building things, getting stuck, breaking things, and eventually figuring out what went wrong. That's probably what keeps me interested in programming.

Outside of coding, I play chess and study mathematics. I like both for a similar reason: you usually have to look at a problem from a few different angles before things start making sense.

Right now, I'm focusing on getting better at web development and Python while slowly learning how AI and machine learning actually work. I'm still at the beginning, but I'm enjoying the process of figuring it out.
